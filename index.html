Firemaking

RuneScape Classic lights regular logs only: drop item 14, then use tinderbox 166 on the ground log. burn logs consumes the regular logs already held; train firemaking continuously chops regular trees and burns the logs. AutoScape retries failed rolls and moves off each fire. Fires use object 97 and eventually leave ashes 181.

Starter kit and safe banking

New characters receive a bronze axe 87, cooked meat 132, tinderbox 166, and bronze pickaxe 156. Existing accounts are preserved. This private world adds a Lumbridge banker, and automation chooses the nearest supported bank for its current position. Quick Bank's Gathered and Loot buttons skip equipped items; Carried deposits every unequipped stack intentionally.

Useful commands

chop 10 logs · mine 20 iron ore · kill 5 skeletons · train strength on chickens · fight chickens to the death · bury 10 bones · burn logs · train firemaking · bank inventory · deposit gathered resources · bank loot · stop

Combat commands can choose Controlled, Strength, Attack, or Defence XP. “To the death” and “never bank” disable combat banking until changed. Chain supported jobs with commas, semicolons, arrows, or then. Examples: kill 10 chickens, then bury 10 bones, chop 10 logs, then firemake them, and mine 20 iron, then bank gathered resources. Banking steps keep equipped gear. Numbered steps complete only after real resources, fires, burials, or kills are confirmed. An unnumbered queued gathering step fills the inventory without banking, then the next step starts automatically. The current step, progress, and remaining queue persist locally after login.
